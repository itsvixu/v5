Specs so they look uniform
- SVG, cropped tight to the mark with no padding.
- Transparent background. The site renders every logo as one flat dark colour, so a logo with a solid square or rectangle behind it will show as a black block.
- Horizontal lock-up (mark plus wordmark) where one exists. They display 28px high, max 168px wide.
- Crests and badges with fine detail (Met Police, Fire Brigade) may need a simplified one-colour version.
