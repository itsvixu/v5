Selected work

Each project shows my part and the team's, the decisions I made, what shipped and what the results said, including what went wrong.

  • Hotels.com, 2025

    Save Your Way

    A way to bank a hotel saving for a later trip, designed with no precedent to copy and almost no chance to test.

    The project I led myself from discovery to launch, on web, iOS and Android.

    The project I kept on my own desk while running four workstreams.

    $505Kbanked as OneKeyCash in the first 28 days

  • Expedia Group, 2022 to 2026

    One Key credit card application

    A credit card application that had to satisfy a bank's legal team and still make sense to the person applying.

    Form design, research cycles and a partner's SDK, decision by decision.

    A bank, a card network, two agencies and a review process I had to design as well.

    120+rounds of legal, risk and compliance review with the issuing bank

  • VIXU Design, 2026

    Building with AI

    A wildlife site for schools that I designed and built on my own, and an agent that tracked competitor card offers.

    What I make now, and how I use AI tools to get from a concept to something that works.

    How I brought AI into a design team's practice and where I kept people in the loop.

    9 in a dayoffer design variants produced with AI. It used to take a week

  • Trainline, earlier work

    Trainline apps and mobile ticketing

    A mobile-first rebuild of the UK's biggest train booking site, and mobile ticketing that was the first of its kind for a UK train ticket provider.

    Native iOS and Android, designed sitting among the engineers.

    First in-house designer. I hired the team that followed and chose the agency.

    8%increase in conversion, measured at the time, from homepage search through to checkout

  • Hotels.com, 2018 to 2022

    A credit card offer inside checkout

    Five rounds of testing on one module, in the most conversion-sensitive page of the site.

    I was the only designer on the card, from the first research brief to the last test.

    Sole designer on the card, working weekly with the issuing bank.

    +12%click-through from an offer banner, with no harm to conversion

  • Hotels.com, 2016 to 2019

    Checkout North Star

    A long-term vision for the booking form, the measurement plan behind it, and three years of tests. Some of them lost.

    I wrote the vision, specified the tracking and designed the tests.

    A vision I wrote, got funded and presented to the leadership team.

    $31.7Mcombined annual gross profit from six winning tests, as modelled by analysts

  • Lloyds Banking Group, earlier work

    Regulated forms, and my one internal tool for expert users.

    The bank's design representative inside an agency team.
